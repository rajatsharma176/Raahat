FILING DEADLINE SUMMARY

Claim Type               | Deadline
-------------------------|------------------
Emergency hospitalization | 30 days post-discharge
Planned hospitalization   | 15 days post-discharge
Accident-related claims   | 30 days from incident
Outpatient               | 15 days from treatment

LATE SUBMISSION POLICY
- Day 31-45: 10% penalty on eligible amount
- Day 46-60: 25% penalty on eligible amount
- Beyond 60 days: Claim may be rejected at insurer's discretion

CLAIM REJECTION REASONS (most common)

1. Missing discharge summary (most frequent rejection cause)
2. Incomplete claim form
3. Filing after deadline
4. Non-covered procedure
5. Pre-existing condition without waiting period completion
6. Incorrect policy details

RESUBMISSION AFTER REJECTION

If your claim is rejected:
1. Review the rejection notice carefully
2. Collect any missing documents
3. Resubmit through the Claims Portal within 15 days of rejection notice
4. Resubmissions are processed within 5-7 business days
