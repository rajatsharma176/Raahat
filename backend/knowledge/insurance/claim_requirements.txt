HOSPITALIZATION CLAIM CHECKLIST

Before submitting your claim, ensure you have ALL of the following:

□ DISCHARGE SUMMARY (MANDATORY)
  - Without this, the claim CANNOT be processed
  - Must include diagnosis, admission date, discharge date
  - Must be signed by treating physician on hospital letterhead

□ ADMISSION NOTE (MANDATORY)
  - Initial assessment and reason for admission
  - Time and date of admission

□ Original hospital bills (for reimbursement claims)

□ Policy holder's government ID

□ Completed HealthCo Claim Form (Form HC-2024-01)

WHY IS THE DISCHARGE SUMMARY MANDATORY?

The discharge summary is required because:
1. It confirms the patient was actually hospitalized
2. It validates the duration of hospitalization
3. It provides the official medical diagnosis
4. It confirms the treatment was medically necessary
5. It is the primary document used to assess claim validity

Missing discharge summary = automatic claim rejection.

HOW TO OBTAIN A DISCHARGE SUMMARY

1. Request from the hospital at the time of discharge
2. If not received at discharge, contact the hospital's Medical Records department
3. Most hospitals can provide discharge summary within 24-48 hours of request
4. Some hospitals provide digital copies via their patient portal

FILING DEADLINES

- Emergency hospitalization: File within 30 days of discharge
- Planned hospitalization: File within 15 days of discharge
- Late filing: Subject to a 10% penalty; claims older than 60 days may be rejected
