SECTION 1: COVERAGE OVERVIEW

1.1 HealthCo Comprehensive Health Insurance covers hospitalization expenses incurred due to:
    - Accident or injury
    - Acute illness requiring inpatient treatment
    - Planned surgical procedures
    - Emergency medical conditions

1.2 Coverage limits:
    - Inpatient hospitalization: Up to ₹5,00,000 per year
    - ICU charges: Up to ₹10,000 per day
    - Ambulance charges: Up to ₹5,000 per incident
    - Pre-hospitalization expenses (30 days): Covered
    - Post-hospitalization expenses (60 days): Covered

1.3 The policy covers both planned and emergency hospitalizations.

SECTION 2: CLAIM PROCESS

2.1 Reimbursement Claims (post-hospitalization)
For expenses already incurred, submit a reimbursement claim within 30 days of discharge.

2.2 Cashless Claims (pre-authorized)
For planned procedures, request pre-authorization at least 48 hours in advance.

2.3 Emergency Claims
For emergency hospitalizations, notify HealthCo within 24 hours of admission.
Claim must be filed within 30 days of discharge.

SECTION 3: REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR HOSPITALIZATION CLAIM

3.1 MANDATORY documents (claim cannot be processed without these):
    a) DISCHARGE SUMMARY - This is the most critical document.
       - Must be on hospital letterhead
       - Must include: diagnosis, dates of admission and discharge, treatment given
       - Must be signed by the treating physician
       - CLAIMS SUBMITTED WITHOUT DISCHARGE SUMMARY WILL BE AUTOMATICALLY REJECTED

    b) ADMISSION NOTE / RECORDS
       - Initial assessment by the treating physician
       - Reason for admission
       - Emergency notes (if applicable)

3.2 SUPPORTING documents (highly recommended, may be required):
    c) Itemized medical bills (all bills from hospital)
    d) Pharmacy receipts (for prescribed medications)
    e) Laboratory test reports
    f) Diagnostic imaging reports (X-rays, MRI, CT scans)
    g) Operation notes (if surgery was performed)

3.3 IDENTIFICATION documents:
    h) Policy holder's ID proof
    i) Hospital registration/accreditation certificate

SECTION 4: CLAIM SUBMISSION

4.1 Collect all required documents before submitting.
4.2 Submit via HealthCo Claims Portal or visiting the nearest branch.
4.3 Incomplete submissions will be returned with a list of missing documents.
4.4 Once complete documents are received, processing begins within 7-10 business days.
