HOW TO APPLY FOR MEDICAL LEAVE

Step 1: Collect medical documents
    - Get a discharge summary from the hospital (for hospitalization)
    - Get a medical certificate from your treating physician
    - Collect any additional supporting documents

Step 2: Submit application
    - Go to Student Services Portal (academic.demouniversity.edu/portal)
    - Navigate to "Leave Applications" > "Medical Leave"
    - Fill in the required details
    - Upload scanned copies of all documents
    - Submit application

Step 3: Verification
    - The Medical Officer will verify the documents within 2 business days
    - You will receive an email confirmation of receipt

Step 4: Approval / Rejection
    - Approved: Your leave is recorded, attendance is condoned
    - Rejected: You will receive reasons and may resubmit with correct documents

Step 5: Follow-up for exam deferral (if applicable)
    - If your leave covers an examination date, separately apply for exam deferral
    - Navigate to "Exam Requests" > "Medical Deferral"
    - Reference your approved medical leave application ID

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Keep originals of all documents for physical verification if requested
- Email submissions are NOT accepted; use the online portal only
- Applications submitted after the 5-day deadline require special approval from the Dean
