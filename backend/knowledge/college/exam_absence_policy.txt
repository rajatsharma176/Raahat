SECTION 1: GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1 All students are expected to appear for scheduled examinations. Missing an examination without prior approval or valid documented reason may result in a grade of zero for that examination.

1.2 This policy recognizes that genuine emergencies may prevent students from appearing for examinations.

1.3 The university provides mechanisms for exam deferral in documented cases of medical emergencies, bereavement, or other exceptional circumstances.

SECTION 2: GROUNDS FOR EXAM DEFERRAL

2.1 MEDICAL EMERGENCY
Students absent due to documented medical emergencies may request exam deferral.
Medical emergency includes:
    - Hospitalization (any duration)
    - Acute illness requiring bed rest certified by a physician
    - Accidents resulting in physical incapacitation
    - Emergency surgery or medical procedures

2.2 BEREAVEMENT
Death of an immediate family member (parent, sibling, spouse, child).
Maximum deferral period: 7 days from the date of bereavement.

2.3 NATURAL DISASTER OR FORCE MAJEURE
Events beyond the student's control affecting their ability to appear.

SECTION 3: APPLICATION PROCESS FOR EXAM DEFERRAL

3.1 Students must submit a deferral request through the Academic Affairs Portal.

3.2 Deferral requests must be submitted BEFORE the examination date if possible, or within 48 hours after the missed examination.

3.3 If the student is hospitalized and unable to submit the request personally, a parent, guardian, or authorized representative may submit on their behalf.

3.4 Required documents for medical deferral:
    a) Medical certificate from a registered practitioner (mandatory)
    b) Hospital discharge summary (mandatory for hospitalization cases)
    c) Admission records (if available)

3.5 Incomplete applications will be rejected. Students will be notified of missing documents and given 5 days to provide them.

SECTION 4: EXAM DATE CHANGE PROVISIONS

4.1 The university reserves the right to change examination dates due to:
    - Scheduling conflicts
    - External events (natural disasters, public emergencies)
    - Technical or administrative reasons

4.2 In case of exam date change, the university will notify students with at least 24 hours' notice.

4.3 IMPORTANT: If an exam date is changed to a date within a student's approved medical leave period, the student's deferral request is automatically extended to cover the new date.

4.4 Students who were already on approved medical leave when an exam date change is announced should be automatically rescheduled. Students should confirm this with the Academic Affairs office.

4.5 If the changed exam date creates a conflict with an ongoing medical absence, the student must notify the Academic Affairs office within 12 hours of the announcement.

SECTION 5: MAKE-UP EXAMINATION SCHEDULE

5.1 Make-up examinations for approved deferrals are conducted 2-3 weeks after the original exam date.

5.2 Students who receive exam deferral approval will be notified of the make-up exam schedule within 5 business days.

5.3 Make-up examination format and difficulty are equivalent to the original examination.

5.4 Only one deferral is permitted per course per semester.

SECTION 6: ATTENDANCE ELIGIBILITY FOR DEFERRAL

6.1 Students must have a minimum of 60% attendance (after medical condonation) to be eligible for exam deferral.

6.2 Students with attendance below 60% even after condonation are not eligible for exam deferral and must re-register for the course.
