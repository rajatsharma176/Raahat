SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS

1.1 All students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for semester examinations.

1.2 Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may be allowed to appear for examinations upon approval of the Dean of Academic Affairs, provided valid reasons are documented.

1.3 Students with attendance below 65% will be considered debarred from examinations unless special dispensation is granted under Section 4 (Medical Emergency Provisions).

1.4 Attendance is calculated based on the total number of classes held in each course.

SECTION 2: MEDICAL LEAVE PROVISIONS

2.1 Students who are absent due to illness, injury, or other documented medical conditions may apply for medical leave.

2.2 Medical leave applications must be submitted within 5 working days of returning to the institution.

2.3 Required documentation for medical leave:
    a) Medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner
    b) Hospital discharge summary (if hospitalized)
    c) Any supporting documents as deemed necessary by the Medical Officer

2.4 Medical leave of up to 30 days per semester may be granted without affecting the student's academic standing, subject to documentation.

2.5 For hospitalization cases: Students hospitalized for more than 2 consecutive days are eligible for medical leave covering the entire hospitalization period plus 3 days of recovery.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION 3: ATTENDANCE CONDONATION

3.1 Students with medical leave absences will have those days condoned (not counted against attendance).

3.2 After condonation, if attendance meets the 75% threshold, the student is eligible for examinations without restriction.

3.3 If post-condonation attendance is between 65-74%, the student may appear for examinations with a written undertaking from a parent/guardian.

3.4 The condonation process must be initiated through the Student Services Portal within 10 days of returning from medical leave.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION 4: MEDICAL EMERGENCY PROVISIONS

4.1 In cases of sudden medical emergencies (accidents, acute illness requiring emergency hospitalization), special provisions apply.

4.2 Students unable to attend classes or examinations due to a verified medical emergency will be granted:
    a) Full attendance condonation for the emergency period
    b) Right to sit for deferred/make-up examinations
    c) Extension of assignment submission deadlines

4.3 Verification process: Emergency medical leaves require submission of:
    - Hospital admission records
    - Discharge summary from treating physician
    - Medical certificate specifying dates of incapacity

4.4 Emergency medical provisions must be invoked within 7 days of the student's return to campus.

SECTION 5: CONDONATION LIMITS

5.1 Maximum condonable absence per semester: 15% of total classes
5.2 Attendance after condonation must not fall below 60%
5.3 A student may not invoke medical condonation in consecutive semesters without review by the Academic Committee
