AVAILABLE MEDICAL DOCUMENTS

1. DISCHARGE SUMMARY
   - Comprehensive summary of hospitalization
   - Includes: diagnosis, treatment, medications, follow-up instructions
   - Signed by the attending physician
   - Processing time: 24-48 hours (emergency requests: same day)

2. ADMISSION NOTE
   - Record of initial patient assessment on admission
   - Includes: presenting complaints, vital signs, initial diagnosis
   - Processing time: Same day

3. MEDICAL CERTIFICATE
   - Certifies period of medical incapacity
   - States dates during which patient was unfit for normal activities
   - Signed by treating physician
   - Processing time: Same day

4. INVESTIGATION REPORTS
   - Blood work, imaging, ECG results
   - Processing time: 1-2 days

HOW TO REQUEST DOCUMENTS

Option 1: Online Portal
    - Access: records.citygeneralhospital.com/portal (DEMO)
    - Login with your Patient ID and date of birth
    - Select required documents
    - Specify purpose (insurance claim / educational institution / personal)

Option 2: In-Person
    - Visit Medical Records Department (Ground Floor, Block A)
    - Bring ID proof and patient registration number
    - Fill Request Form MR-2024

Option 3: Authorized Representative
    - If patient is unable to request personally, an authorized representative may do so
    - Requires: Authorization letter, representative's ID, patient's ID copy
