MANAGING FINANCIAL COMMITMENTS DURING MEDICAL EMERGENCIES

This document provides general guidance (not financial advice) on managing
financial obligations during a period of medical incapacitation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RENT OBLIGATIONS

1. Most landlords understand genuine medical emergencies.
2. Communicate proactively with your landlord.
3. Request a brief extension (typically 5-7 days).
4. Provide documentation of hospitalization.
5. Many landlords will accommodate if notified in advance.
6. Check rental agreement for late payment clauses.

PRIORITY: HIGH - Rent is a critical obligation affecting housing security.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CREDIT CARD OBLIGATIONS

1. Contact your credit card issuer's customer service immediately.
2. Explain the medical emergency and request a temporary extension.
3. Most major card issuers have hardship programs.
4. Request: waiver of late fees due to medical emergency.
5. Provide documentation if requested.
6. Minimum payments should be prioritized over full payments.

PRIORITY: HIGH - Late payments affect credit score significantly.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LOAN EMIs

1. Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines allow moratorium requests for genuine hardship.
2. Contact your loan servicer within 5 days of the emergency.
3. EMI moratorium typically covers 1-3 months.
4. Interest continues to accrue during moratorium.
5. Documentation required: hospitalization proof.

PRIORITY: MEDIUM - Banks typically have structured relief programs.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PAYMENT PRIORITIZATION FRAMEWORK

TIER 1 (Critical - Never miss):
  - Rent (housing security)
  - Utility bills (power, water)

TIER 2 (High - Contact for extension):
  - Credit card minimum payments
  - Loan EMIs

TIER 3 (Medium - Usually flexible):
  - Subscriptions
  - Non-essential payments
